Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Key Commitment: ReadmeCraft operates 100% in your web browser. We do not operate databases, user accounts, or backends. Your Personal Access Tokens and profile drafts never leave your device except when interacting directly with GitHub's official APIs.

1. Overview & Data Fiduciary Notice

This Privacy Policy governs your use of ReadmeCraft ("we", "us", or "the Service"). We are committed to transparency and compliance with applicable data protection laws, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and global privacy standards.

2. Data We Process and Why

Because ReadmeCraft is a client-side browser application, data processing occurs locally on your machine:

  • GitHub Public Profile Data: When you enter a GitHub username, the app fetches public information (avatar, name, bio, public repositories) from api.github.com using standard unauthenticated REST requests. This data is cached in your browser's localStorage for up to 10 minutes to prevent API rate limiting.
  • Profile Drafts & Preferences: Your customized modules, bio notes, tech stack selections, and UI theme preferences are stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage. They are never transmitted to our servers or third parties.
  • GitHub Personal Access Tokens (PAT): When using the Publish feature, you may provide a token. This token is held strictly in volatile browser RAM. It is never written to localStorage, never logged, and transmitted exclusively to https://api.github.com to commit your README file. Closing the modal or refreshing the page purges the token from memory immediately.

3. Lawful Basis and India DPDP Act Compliance

In accordance with India's DPDP Act, 2023:

  • Specified Purpose: Any processing of personal data is carried out solely for the explicit purpose of generating and publishing your GitHub profile README file.
  • Consent: Explicit notice and consent mechanisms are provided prior to API calls or token transmission.
  • Right to Withdraw & Erasure: You can purge all stored drafts and cached data instantly at any time by clicking "Clear Draft" or clearing your browser's site data.
  • Children's Data: ReadmeCraft does not track, profile, or collect personal data from minors.

4. Third-Party Services, Analytics & Advertising

To maintain ReadmeCraft as a free tool and continuously improve usability, we utilize select third-party services:

  • Microsoft Clarity: We partner with Microsoft Clarity to capture how you use and interact with our website through behavioral metrics, heatmaps, and session replay to improve user experience. Website usage data is captured using first and third-party cookies and other tracking technologies. For more information about how Microsoft collects and uses your data, visit the Microsoft Privacy Statement.
  • Google AdSense: Google and third-party vendors use cookies to serve non-intrusive ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

5. Your Privacy Rights

Under the DPDP Act and GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of personal data processed (observable at all times in your editor).
  • Correct, update, or erase your data (via the in-app controls or browser storage settings).
  • Seek grievance redressal regarding any data-related concern.

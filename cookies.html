Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Last Revised: November 19, 2026

1. What are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. Local Storage is a modern web standard that allows applications to store key-value data directly in your browser with higher capacity and zero overhead.

2. How ReadmeCraft Uses Local Storage

ReadmeCraft does not use proprietary tracking cookies. We utilize strictly necessary browser localStorage to deliver core application functionality:

Storage Key Purpose Duration
readmecraft_draft_v1 Stores your active modules, section content, and settings so your work is preserved across refreshes. Persistent (until cleared)
readmecraft_theme_v1 Remembers your chosen UI appearance (System / Dark / Light mode). Persistent
readmecraft_gh_cache_v1 Caches public GitHub profile and repository queries to avoid GitHub unauthenticated rate limits. 10 Minutes
readmecraft_consent_v1 Records your acknowledgement of the cookie and privacy notice. 12 Months

3. Third-Party Analytics & Advertising Cookies

We partner with Microsoft Clarity and Google AdSense to capture website usage analytics and display non-intrusive advertisements:

  • Microsoft Clarity: Sets first and third-party cookies (such as _clck and _clsk) to record anonymized interaction metrics, scroll depth, and session replay to help us understand user workflows and debug frontend friction points.
  • Google AdSense: Google and its ad technology partners may place and read cookies on your browser to serve relevant ads based on prior visits.

4. How to Control or Delete Cookies

You have full control over browser storage and cookies:

  • Opt-out of Personalized Ads: Visit Google Ads Settings.
  • Browser Cookie Settings: You can configure your browser to block or alert you about cookies. Note that blocking essential Local Storage will prevent drafts from auto-saving.
  • Clear Local Storage: You can clear your draft at any time by clicking "Clear Draft" inside the editor or via your browser developer tools (Storage > Local Storage).

5. Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal obligations. Please review this page periodically.
